# Sovranly IP Blueprint

## 1. Project Overview
Sovranly IP is a blockchain-based marketplace dedicated to revolutionizing how artists manage and monetize their intellectual property. The platform is a decentralized space where artists exercise complete control over their creations.

## 2. Core Features
* **Licensing and Rights Management**: Creators set licensing terms, pricing, and usage rights for their intellectual property.
* **Direct Sales and Transactions**: Buyers directly purchase or license content from creators using cryptocurrency.
* **Automated Royalty Payments**: Smart contracts automatically distribute royalties upon each transaction, ensuring fair and timely compensation.
* **Transparency and Tracking**: All transactions, licensing agreements, and royalty distributions are recorded on the blockchain, providing complete transparency.
* **Zero Trust Architecture**: Ensuring all sessions and data accesses are authenticated, authorized, and continuously validated.

## 3. Target Clients
* **Artists**: Visual artists, digital creators, photographers, and sculptors.
* **Musicians**: Independent musicians, composers, and bands.
* **Writers**: Authors, poets, screenwriters, and journalists.
* **Software Developers**: Independent software engineers, open-source contributors, game developers, and tech builders looking to license codebases, modules, custom algorithms, or training weights.

## 4. Technology Stack (Proposed)
* **Blockchain/Smart Contracts**: EVM compatible (e.g., Polygon, Ethereum). Solidity.
* **Front-End**: Next.js (App Router), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Recharts.
* **Back-End**: Node.js, Express (if needed for complex backend tasks), Drizzle ORM.
* **Database**: PostgreSQL (via Drizzle).
* **Payment/Identity**: Zero Trust Auth patterns, Wallet connections (MetaMask/Web3).

## 5. Vision
Establish Sovranly IP as the premier destination for artists seeking control, transparency, and fair compensation in the digital age, enabling them to thrive directly connected to their audiences.
